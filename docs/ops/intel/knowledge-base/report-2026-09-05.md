# Product Intel Report — 2026-09-05 (deep cycle)

## 1. Executive summary
This cycle (2026-09-05) represents a **DEEP** intelligence run, focusing on broadening the competitive landscape and refreshing existing profiles. 

The market is rapidly bifurcating into two distinct segments:
1.  **Infrastructure/Gateway (Control Plane):** Companies like **TrueFoundry** and **Protecto** are building robust, centralized gateways for LLMs, MCPs, and agents. They focus on routing, observability, and centralized policy enforcement.
2.  **Agent/Endpoint Security (Runtime & Posture):** Companies like **Straiker**, **Harmonic Security**, and **Nightfall AI** are focusing on "Agent-SPM" (Security Post/Posture Management), "Shadow AI" detection, and endpoint-based runtime protection (browser extensions/agents).

**Key Strategic Finding:** There is a high degree of "feature overlap" appearing between the gateway layer and the endpoint layer. The emergence of **MCP (Model Context Protocol) security** as a specific battleground is the most critical new dimension. Our "zero-server" client-side/endpoint approach (PrivacyScrubber) remains highly defensible against the "cloud-centric"/ "gateway-centric" architectures of the identified competitors, particularly for high-sovereignty (DACH/KRITIS) use cases.

## 2. Changes observed this cycle
*   **Protecto:** Re-verified positioning and feature set. Confirmed high accuracy claims (99.9%) and presence in the "agentic" space via MCP and RAG support.
*   **Straiker:** Re-verified the "three pillars" (Discover/Ascend/Defend) and the specific focus on **MCP Server & Claude Skills discovery**.
*   **Harmonic Security:** Re-verified the specialized "endpoint SLM" technology for inline redaction, which remains our most direct technical competitor for the "redaction" feature, but with a different deployment model (Managed/Agent-based vs. Zero-Server).
*   **Nightfall AI:** Re-verified "Hooks-level interception" capabilities for coding agents (e.g., Claude Code) and the "Nyx" autonomous analyst agent.
*   **TrueFoundry:** Re-verified the expansion of the **TrueForge** agent harness and the emphasis on being an "open-source, vendor-neutral" infrastructure player.

**Strategic Read:**
1.  **The "MCP War" is starting:** Every major player is now claiming "MCP security" or "MCP discovery." We must accelerate our MCP-layer interception/sanitization capabilities.
2.  **Endpoint vs. Gateway:** The market is splitting. We should avoid competing for "Cloud Gateway" budget (TrueFoundry/Protecto territory) and instead double down on the "Endpoint/Browser" (Harmonic/Nightfall) and "Developer/IDE" (Nightfall/Straiker) surfaces where we have a structural advantage via zero-server privacy.
3.  **The "Agent-SPM" Vocabulary:** "Agent Security Posture Management" is the new industry standard term. Our marketing/product documentation should adopt this terminology.
